# Privacy Policy for EarSwitch

**Last Updated:** September 2026

**EarSwitch** is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how EarSwitch handles information and demonstrates our commitment to local data processing and user confidentiality.

---

## 1. No Data Collection or Transmission
EarSwitch is a standalone desktop audio management utility that operates strictly locally on your machine.
- **No Automatic Data Collection:** EarSwitch does **not** collect, monitor, track, or transmit any user activity, browsing history, or audio/microphone streams.
- **No Automatic Crash Reporting:** EarSwitch does **not** automatically upload or transmit crash reports, stack traces, or error diagnostics to any remote server or third party.
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect, request, or store personal identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, IP addresses, or device locations).

---

## 2. 100% Local Configuration & Storage
All settings and application configurations are saved **locally on your computer** and never leave your device. This includes:
- Per-application volume and mute preferences
- Default audio playback and recording device selections
- Custom keyboard shortcuts and hotkey configurations
- Theme preferences and tray icon style choices

---

## 3. Local Diagnostics
Any diagnostic logs generated for troubleshooting purposes are retained **only in local memory / local system logs** on your PC. No telemetry or diagnostics are sent across the network.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising
- **Zero Third-Party Sharing:** EarSwitch does not partner with advertisers or third-party analytics services.
- **No Ads or Trackers:** There are no advertisements, analytical SDKs, or background trackers embedded in the application.
