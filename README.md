# EarSwitch

A modern, fast, and intuitive Windows 11 Fluent Audio & Volume Management utility. EarSwitch allows you to take total control over your system and per-application sound levels with seamless device switching.

---

## 🎧 Features

- **Fluent 2 Modern Interface:** Crafted with native Windows 11 visuals, dark/light mode support, and smooth, flicker-free animations.
- **Per-Application Volume Control:** Independently adjust the volume and mute state for any running app.
- **Quick Audio Output Switching:** Switch default audio playback and communication devices instantly from the flyout or system tray.
- **Customizable Tray Icons:** Choose from multiple modern tray icon styles (Headphones, Audio Waves, Treble Clef, Saxophone, Dynamic).
- **Keyboard Shortcuts & Hotkeys:** Assign custom keybindings to quickly manage master and per-app sound levels.
- **100% Local & Private:** No telemetry tracking, no automatic crash reporting, and zero external network transmission. All data stays strictly on your computer.
